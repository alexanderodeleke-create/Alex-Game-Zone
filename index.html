HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Target mode: Tap targets to score. Each target left untouched costs one life. Clear targets quickly to get a high score.

🏃 Survival mode: Use ◀ and ▶ or tap the left/right half of the arena to move. Avoid falling blocks. Survive as long as you can.

⚡ Levels: Level 1 is the easiest; level 5 is the hardest.

Tip: rotate your phone sideways for a wider play area. Sound may start after your first tap due to browser rules.

Android home-screen shortcut: open this game in Chrome from a hosted website, tap ⋮, then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app” if offered. This package includes installable-web-app files. Host it over HTTPS to enable installation; shared online multiplayer/leaderboards still need a server.
